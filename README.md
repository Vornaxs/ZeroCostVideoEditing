## Free-plan notes

**Free and open-source software:** OpenShot, Shotcut, Kdenlive, and Blender.

Other tools may offer free plans, limited access, trials, or paid upgrades. DaVinci Resolve has a free edition and an optional paid Studio edition.

Before choosing an editor, check:
- Export resolution and watermark restrictions
- Premium templates, effects, or assets
- Monthly usage or storage limits
- Trial length and subscription requirements
- Supported operating systems

1. Download from the official links above.
2. Check system requirements and supported platforms.
3. Review current pricing before starting a project.
4. Avoid unofficial download mirrors.
5. Back up your project files.
